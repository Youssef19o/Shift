SHIFT

Survive the converging swarm. Switch colors, absorb matching shapes, build combos and keep moving — camp on one color and the opposite hunters will home in.

Mouse: left-click = White, right-click (or Ctrl-click) = Black
Keyboard / trackpad: Space or Shift toggles color
Touch: drag to move, tap the SHIFT button
Esc / P pauses

Best score: 0

HOW TO PLAY

Colors. Pass through shapes matching your color to absorb them. Opposite colors cost a life. Grey shapes always hurt.
Energy. Each shift costs 20 energy (regenerates over time; absorbing refills a little). No energy, no shift.
Combos. Chain absorbs within 2.5 s to raise your multiplier up to x8. Getting hit resets it.
Telegraphs. A pulsing ring at the screen edge shows where a shape is about to enter.
Enemies. Darts are fast, orbiters weave, yellow-ringed splitters break in two. Waves arrive as a line of one color.
Pickups. Coins: every 10 raises the tier (faster and bigger, capped). S = shield, ♥ = life, ◔ = slow-mo, ✸ = clear screen, M = magnet.
Camping. Stay on one color over 5 s and opposite shapes home in. Shapes also aim at you, so corners are no refuge.
